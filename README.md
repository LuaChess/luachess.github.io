## ♟️ About

LuaChess is a free chess site that runs entirely in your browser. There is no account to create, nothing to install and no server of ours to wait on. Open the page, choose a time control and start playing.

It is built around a simple loop: **play, win, gain rating, earn a rank**. The bot you face is matched to your current rating, so games stay close. Everything else, from the board colors to the way a king is checkmated, is up to you.

## 🚀 Quick start

1. Open **[luachess.github.io](https://luachess.github.io/)**.
2. Go to **Play** and choose **Play Online** (a rating-matched opponent) or **Play Bots**.
3. Pick a mode: **Bullet**, **Blitz** or **Rapid**. Your rating starts at **100** and goes up as you win.

New to chess? Open **Puzzles** first. Each solved puzzle unlocks the next one.

## ✨ Features

### Play

| | |
|---|---|
| ⏱️ **Three time controls** | Bullet (1 min), Blitz (3 min) and Rapid (10 min), each with its own separate rating. |
| 🌐 **Play Online** | Press one button and get matched with an opponent of similar skill. |
| 🤖 **Rating-matched bot** | The opponent scales with your rating. Weaker bots look one move ahead and make deliberate mistakes; stronger ones search deeper. |
| 🎭 **Play Bots** | Challenge named bots with their own personality and chat bubbles: Shulkified (400), Alex (800) and StockFish (4000). Bot games are unrated. |
| 🎲 **Variants** | Standard chess, **Chess960** (randomized starting position, with full 960 castling) and **3 Check** (give check three times to win). |
| 📋 **Full chess rules** | Legal-move checking, castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, the 50-move rule and insufficient material. |
| 👑 **Pawn promotion** | Pick a queen, rook, bishop or knight when a pawn reaches the last rank. |
| ⚡ **Premoves** | Optional. Queue your next move while the bot is thinking. Click the pre-moved piece to cancel it. |
| 💾 **Resume your game** | Close the page in the middle of a game and pick up exactly where you left off (when you accept the privacy notice). |
| 🔁 **Next round** | Jump straight back to the lobby after each result. |
| 🏳️ **Resign** | Give up a game at any time. |

### Puzzles

| | |
|---|---|
| 🧩 **30+ guided puzzles** | Three sections: Basics, Easy and Intermediate. Captures, forks, skewers, pins, checkmates and special rules like en passant, castling and promotion. |
| 📖 **Opening lessons** | Play through the Italian Game, London System, Queen's Gambit, Ruy Lopez, Evans Gambit, Caro-Kann, Sicilian, French, King's Indian and more, plus traps like Scholar's Mate, Legal's Mate and the Fried Liver Attack. |
| 🔓 **Progressive unlock** | Solve a puzzle to unlock the next one. Progress is saved. |
| 💡 **Hints and restart** | Ask for a hint or restart any puzzle. Wrong moves slide back so you can try again. |
| 👾 **Boss fights** | Beat the bots in a full game on the puzzle board. |

### Learn and review

| | |
|---|---|
| 📜 **Move list** | Every move is recorded in standard notation as you play. |
| 📖 **Opening names** | The current opening is shown above the move list, from the King's Pawn to the Queen's Gambit and many more. |
| ⏮️ **Game review** | Step through any finished game with the on-screen buttons or the left and right arrow keys, with the same animations as in play. |
| 🏅 **Move ratings** | Reviewed moves are labeled Brilliant, Great, Best, Excellent, Good, Book, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss or Blunder. |
| 📊 **Play rating estimate** | The review estimates how strongly both sides played, based on the quality of their moves. |
| 🏹 **Board arrows** | Right-click and drag to draw arrows and circles on the board. |
| ♟️ **Captured pieces** | See what each side has taken and who is ahead in material. |
| 🧪 **Simulation mode** | A free sandbox board where you move both sides, with undo, flip and reset. It supports Standard, Chess960 and 3 Check. |

## 🎨 Customization

Open **Settings** from the sidebar. Everything is grouped into tabs:

- **Board:** 19 themes, or pick your own light and dark square colors.
- **Pieces:** 8 piece styles, including Blindfold mode for an extra challenge.
- **Moves:** how pieces travel between squares, with a live preview.
- **Check:** how your king reacts to being in check.
- **Checkmate:** 16 animations for the end of a game. Hover to preview and click to choose.
- **Sound:** the sound played on each move.
- **Game:** gameplay options such as premoves.
- **Style:** color presets and a custom CSS editor with upload and download.
- **Fonts:** font presets or your own font file.

## 🔒 Privacy and saved data

LuaChess has no backend and no account system. Your data is never sent to a server. If you accept the notice shown on your first visit, the site stores the following **in your own browser** using `localStorage`:

- Your Bullet, Blitz and Rapid ratings
- Your game history (results, opponents, moves, dates)
- Your profile name and picture
- Your settings (board colors, piece style, animations, sounds, style and font)
- Your puzzle progress
- Your unfinished game, so you can resume it
- A note that you accepted the notice

Choose **Reject All** and the site still works, but your progress will not be saved. Clearing your browser data removes everything the site has stored.

The page does load some public assets from other hosts: piece images and sounds from Chess.com's CDN, web fonts from Google Fonts when you pick a font preset, the public Chess.com streamers list for bot names and pictures, and a QR scanner library from jsDelivr when you scan a login code. None of your game data is part of those requests.

## 🧠 How it works

- **Engine:** a self-contained move generator and alpha-beta search written in plain JavaScript. The bot's search depth and amount of randomness depend on its rating, which is what makes low-rated bots beatable and high-rated bots sharper.
- **Rendering:** the board, pieces, effects and animations are built with HTML, CSS and the Web Animations API.
- **Audio:** move sounds are played from audio files, with a built-in Web Audio fallback.
- **QR login:** your account data is compressed and encoded into a QR code, and decoded on the other device. Nothing leaves your devices.
- **Dependencies:** none to build or install. No build step, no framework, no package manager.

## 💻 Run it locally

```bash
git clone https://github.com/LuaChess/luachess.github.io.git
cd luachess.github.io
```

Then open `index.html` in your browser, or serve the folder with any static server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# open http://localhost:8000
```
