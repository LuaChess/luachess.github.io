## ♟️ About

LuaChess is a chess website built to make playing and exploring chess feel straightforward. It features a dark interface, a playable chessboard, a move list, player ratings, and configurable game settings, all directly in the browser.

## ✨ Features

- **⏱️ Time controls:** Choose between Bullet, Blitz, and Rapid.
- **♟️ Play against a bot:** Play against a bot with a selectable rating.
- **🎨 Customization:** Adjust the board and piece appearance through settings.
- **♛ Pawn promotion:** Choose a queen, rook, bishop, or knight when promoting.
- **📜 Move history:** Follow the moves made during a game and review positions.
- **💥 Checkmate effects:** Animated effects make the end of a game feel more dramatic.
- **👤 Player profile:** View your profile and rating information.
- **📱 Responsive layout:** Play on desktop or smaller screens.

## 🚀 Play

Open **[luachess.github.io](https://luachess.github.io/)** in your browser. No installation is needed.
